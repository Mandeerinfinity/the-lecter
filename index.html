Mouse: the scroll wheel turns the bezel, the pushers at 2 and 4 o'clock run the chronograph, the crown moves to the next complication, the slide on the left flank strikes the minute repeater, and a double-click turns the watch over. Some things happen only if you are persistent. Touch: swipe to change complication, long-press the crystal to breathe on it, and tilt the device to move the light and swing the rotor.
