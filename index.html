Keyboard & gestures

←→ or []Turn the bezel to the previous or next complication
1–9, 0Jump to complications 1 to 10
SpaceStart or stop the chronograph. In Music it plays or pauses
L / RLap or reset (Chronograph)
T / Shift+TNext or previous dial
KTurn the watch over to see the case back and movement
BBreathe on the glass, then wipe it with the cursor
MSummon or dismiss the moths
PPlay or pause the harpsichord from anywhere
HSwitch between 12- and 24-hour time
FFullscreen
Y / NAnswer yes or no (Interrogation)
? · EscShow this card · close whatever is open

Mouse: the scroll wheel turns the bezel, the pushers at 2 and 4 o'clock run the chronograph, the crown moves to the next complication, and a double-click turns the watch over. Touch: swipe to change complication, long-press the crystal to breathe on it, and tilt the device to move the light and swing the rotor.
